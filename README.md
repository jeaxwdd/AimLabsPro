**AimLabsPro** is a lightweight, high-performance, browser-based FPS aim trainer designed for instant warm-ups and precision practice—no installation or complex setup required. 

Warm up before your competitive matches, or push your **flicking**, **tracking**, and **reaction speed** to the next level! 🎮⚡

---

## 🔥 Key Features

* ⚡ **Instant Launch:** Run `PLAY AIMLABS.bat` with a single click to start the local server and play directly in your browser.
* 🎯 **Multiple Training Modes:** Test your reflexes with flicking, tracking, and target precision challenges.
* 🚀 **Zero Lag:** Lightweight browser engine optimized for high FPS, even on lower-end systems.
* 📊 **Detailed Stats:** Track your accuracy percentage, reaction times, and high scores.
* 🛠️ **Customizable Settings:** Tweak in-game mouse sensitivity and crosshair styles to match your favorite games.
